about

Kat Sophia is an LA-based artist who fuses singer-songwriter sensibilities with indie pop. Through her music, she writes lyrics that explore the complex layers of love and emotional extremes through storytelling, and wants to put them to music that feels raw, whether that be through extremely crooked uses of indie and electronic sounds, or just her guitar.

Deeply embedded in her local music scene, Kat is currently learning production from and being mentored by a producer of artists including Halsey, Dominic Fike and Lil Nas X. She also covers major acts like Chappell Roan, Taylor Swift, and The Weeknd as a music journalist and photographer.

Her goal is to make music that makes you want to both cry and laugh, preferably at the same time.
